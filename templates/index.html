How to Add a Gateway

Connect a LoRaWAN gateway to the Helium network by pointing its packet forwarder at the multi-gateway aggregator:

server_address: gateway.sattrack.co.uk
port: 1700

A keypair is auto-provisioned on first connection. The gateway will connect as a new Hotspot on the Helium network. Each gateway must have a unique Gateway EUI — check your packet forwarder config and replace the default with your concentrator's actual EUI.
